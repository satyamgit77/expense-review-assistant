# Organization Expense Policy

## 1. General Rules

### [1.1] Submission Window
Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date. Claims with a future date are not accepted.

### [1.2] Receipts
A receipt is required for every expense above ₹500. Claims above this amount without a receipt cannot be approved until a receipt is provided.

### [1.3] Currency
All limits in this policy are stated in INR. Claims in other currencies require reviewer attention.

## 2. Travel

### [2.1] Travel Limit
Travel expenses (cab, taxi, train, flight tickets, fuel) up to ₹5,000 are permitted per claim. Travel must be for official business purposes.

### [2.2] Travel Details
Every travel claim should state the destination, the purpose of travel, and the mode of transportation.

## 3. Meals

### [3.1] Employee Meals
Meals taken by an employee during official work or business travel are reimbursable.

### [3.2] Meal Limit
Meal expenses are limited to ₹1,500 per claim.

## 4. Accommodation

### [4.1] Accommodation Limit
Hotel and lodging expenses are limited to ₹6,000 per night. Each claim covers one night of stay.

## 5. Office Supplies

### [5.1] Office Supplies Limit
Office supplies (stationery, small equipment, consumables) are limited to ₹3,000 per claim.

## 6. Client Entertainment

### [6.1] Client Entertainment Limit
Meals or events hosted for clients or business partners are classified as Client Entertainment, not as employee meals. The limit is ₹4,000 per claim.

### [6.2] Client Entertainment Details
The claim should state the client or company name and the business purpose of the event.
